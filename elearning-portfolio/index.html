Rural Generalist Nurse — Comprehensive Assessment

Client: District HETI (Health Education and Training Institute NSW)

Project: Designed and developed a gamified simulation e-learning module in which the player takes on the role of a nurse at a rural hospital, responsible for keeping patients alive across an 8-hour shift. The player follows clinical procedure to assess and treat patients, and escalates to a doctor when a treatment falls outside their scope of practice. Each in-game action consumes game time and earns or costs points depending on whether it was clinically appropriate.

Awards:

  • Silver, Brandon Hall Group Excellence in Learning Awards 2017 — Best Use of Games and Simulations for Learning
  • Gold, LearnX Impact Awards 2017 — Best e-Learning Design, Game Design
  • Finalist, Serious Games Showcase and Challenge Australasia (SGSCA), Australasian Simulation Congress 2017

Our role: Front-end development

Ausgrid: Refresher Course for Polarity Testing

Client: Ausgrid

Project: Designed and developed a multi-award-winning learning solution to reduce accidents and near misses among electrically qualified staff and apprentices performing polarity tests across Ausgrid's network. The module interfaces with a physical simulation board (connected via USB) fitted with a button box and test probes matching field equipment, letting learners work through assessment scenarios in a realistic simulated environment tailored to specific job tasks.

Awards:

  • Gold, Brandon Hall Group Excellence in Learning Awards 2014 — Best Custom Content
  • Gold, LearnX Impact Awards 2014 — Best e-Learning Model, Bespoke/Custom
  • Highly Commended, AITD National Training Excellence Awards 2014 — Australian Learning Innovation

Our role: UI/UX design, front-end development
