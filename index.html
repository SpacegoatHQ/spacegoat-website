AREA-01What we do

We specialise in two things and do both properly. Everything else we offer connects back to these.

Digital Accessibility Consulting

We find what's broken, explain why it matters, and help you fix it.

Capabilities:

  • Design review in the early stages of development to prevent accessibility issues from the start
  • End-to-end WCAG 2.1/2.2 Level AA accessibility audits of websites and web applications
  • Automated testing with axe and Accessibility Insights, validated manually (we don't just run a scanner and call it done)
  • Manual keyboard, focus, structure and interaction testing, plus assistive technology testing with JAWS, NVDA, VoiceOver and TalkBack
  • Best practice recommendations for better user experience
  • Detailed audit reports with severity ratings, user impact assessments and practical remediation recommendations, including code examples
  • Remediation retesting: we check the fixes actually work
  • Experience across enterprise SaaS, government, healthcare, financial services and community organisations
  • Complex component-level assessment: custom interfaces, shared design system components, nested interactions

eLearning Development

Custom digital learning that's built to engage, not just tick a box.

Capabilities:

  • Rapid eLearning development in Articulate Storyline, Rise and Evolve Authoring
  • Bespoke eLearning built from scratch in HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript for projects that need it: simulations, games and complex logic, SCORM and xAPI ready
  • Custom widgets and LMS extensions, including D2L
  • Gamification and simulation: branching scenarios, scoring and realistic interactive exercises
  • Built with accessibility in mind from the start
  • Straight advice on which approach suits your project, even when it isn't the one that earns us the most work

Front-end Development & UI/UX Design

We also take on selected front-end development and UI/UX design projects, particularly where they intersect with our accessibility and eLearning work. If you need a component built properly, an interface reviewed, or a prototype that actually reflects how something should behave, we can help.

AREA 04The person behind the work

Peter Moricz is the founder and director of SPACEGOAT Interactive, a Sydney-based digital studio specialising in digital accessibility and eLearning.

He has extensive experience across front-end development, UI/UX design and eLearning. He has spent his career building digital products for real people, and caring about whether they actually work. Over the years, accessibility has become his main focus: auditing websites and web applications across enterprise SaaS platforms, government agencies, healthcare providers and financial services organisations, from complex custom components to shared design system elements.

That development and design background is what makes the findings useful. Peter can think like a developer while auditing like a specialist, so the recommendations are practical, specific and something designers and developers can act on. The through-line is always the same: find what's broken, understand why, and explain it clearly.

On the eLearning side, the work spans award-winning simulations, gamified learning, large-scale module production and custom HTML/CSS/JavaScript development for some of Australia's most recognisable organisations. The same attention to detail goes into every course build.

Outside of work, Peter is a keen traveller and enthusiastic home cook, and spends an arguably unreasonable amount of time playing Dungeons & Dragons with his kids and friends. No apologies.
